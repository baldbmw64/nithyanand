Good to know
Frequently asked
questions.

    

      
How far in advance should I enquire?
The earlier, the better – especially for weddings and weekend celebrations. Just get in touch; we are happy to check short-notice dates too.

      
Can you cater for allergies?
Yes. Every dish on our menu is labelled with the 14 major allergens. List your guests’ allergies and intolerances in your enquiry – we will tailor the menu accordingly.

      
Are there vegetarian and vegan options?
South Indian cuisine is naturally rich in vegetarian dishes – they make up more than half of our menu. For vegan requests, please talk to us directly.

      
Where do you deliver?
We deliver in Frankfurt and the surrounding area. Simply include your postcode in the enquiry.

      
How much does catering cost?
That depends on the format, number of guests and menu. After your enquiry you will receive a personal, transparent quotation.
