Privacy policy

This is an English translation of our German privacy policy. The German version is legally binding.

Protecting your data matters to us. This website uses no tracking, no advertising cookies and no analytics tools. Below we explain which data is processed when you visit it.

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested and browser and system information (server log files). This is required to deliver the website and keep it secure.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably). Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: GitHub privacy statement.

3. Fonts

The fonts used on this website are hosted locally on our server. No connection to third-party servers (e.g. Google) is made when you load the pages.

4. Local storage in your browser

We do not use cookies. For individual functions we store small entries in your browser's local storage (localStorage), e.g. the dishes you save on the menu. This data does not leave your device and is not transmitted to us. Storage is strictly necessary for the function you request (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete these entries at any time in your browser settings.

5. Enquiry form (catering & celebrations)

If you contact us via the enquiry form, we process the data you enter: occasion, date, time, number of guests, format, venue or postcode, requested services and dishes, allergies and wishes, as well as your name, email address and – optionally – phone number. We use this data solely to handle your enquiry and send you a quote.

To forward the form data to us by email we use the service Web3Forms (web3forms.com). The data is transmitted to Web3Forms' servers and forwarded to us from there as an email. Processing outside the EU, in particular in the USA, cannot be ruled out. More information: Web3Forms privacy notice.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract at your request) and your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you give in the form and may withdraw at any time with effect for the future. We delete your enquiry once it has been fully handled, unless statutory retention obligations apply (e.g. if an order is placed).

6. Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for this purpose and no retention obligations apply.

7. Links to other websites

This website contains links to external services (e.g. reservation or social media platforms). You only leave our website when you click such a link; the respective provider is responsible for any data processing there.

8. Your rights

You have the right at any time to

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Simply contact us using the details above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority responsible for us: Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Gustav-Stresemann-Ring 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany.
